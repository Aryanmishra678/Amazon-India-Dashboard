This dashboard analyzes 10,000 Amazon India orders (Jan 2024–Aug 2026) to help management track sales, profit, and order performance. It covers:

Overall performance – total sales, profit, orders, units, average order value, and profit margin, plus a monthly sales-and-profit trend
Products & categories – top and lowest-selling products, and category-level sales, profit, and unit breakdowns
Orders & losses – order status (delivered, shipped, returned, cancelled), return/cancellation rates, and estimated profit lost to returns
Payment, fulfilment & geography – performance by payment method, fulfilment channel, and state

All figures update using the dropdown filters at the top (Year, Category, State, Payment Method, Fulfilment Method).
